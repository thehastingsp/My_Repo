Do Your Homework Before You Choose

Choosing a care facility is one of the most important decisions you will make for yourself or your loved one. We strongly encourage you to research any facility thoroughly before making a commitment.

1

Check State Inspection Reports

The State Department of Aging conducts regular inspections of all licensed care facilities. Request the most recent inspection report for any facility you are considering.

2

Visit in Person

Schedule a tour at different times of day. Observe how staff interact with residents and whether the environment feels welcoming and safe.

3

Ask About Staff Ratios

Find out how many caregivers are on duty during each shift. A lower resident-to-staff ratio generally means more personalized attention.

4

Understand the Costs

Ask for a complete breakdown of fees. Find out what is included in the base rate and what costs extra. Check if they accept Medicaid or offer financial assistance.

5

Talk to Current Families

Ask the facility to connect you with families of current residents. Their firsthand experience can provide valuable insight.
